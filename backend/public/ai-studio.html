AI Studio

Five tools to describe, price, reuse and find textile waste. Every result is labelled as Live AI (answered by Claude) or Simulated (fixed rules and a sample price table).

Engine

What is live and what is simulated

FeatureHow it works nowIn a production version
Fabric recognitionClaude looks at the photo when a Claude connection is available. Otherwise a simple colour and texture check runs in your browser.A vision model trained on labelled textile photos, plus supplier data.
Value estimateA fixed sample price table with condition and bulk factors. Claude can add a short second opinion on request.Real price history from completed sales on the platform.
Upcycling ideasCurated idea lists. Claude can generate extra ideas on request.Ideas linked to local makers and buyers.
Smart matchingWeighted rules on fabric, price, quantity, city and condition. Claude can re-rank on request.A recommender that learns from searches, saves and orders.
Search assistantClaude turns your sentence into filters when allowed. Otherwise a keyword and pattern parser does it, including Arabic city and fabric names.Semantic search over listing text and photos.

Claude can make mistakes and cannot confirm fibre content from a photo. Always check labels with the supplier. Prices and impact numbers are placeholders.
